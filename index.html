From the Tandoor

Our Bread

Every loaf is shaped by hand and slapped onto the searing clay walls of our tandoor, the same way it has been made in Iran for generations.

Taftoon

A soft, thin flatbread with a delicately blistered surface, baked in seconds against the tandoor’s scorching clay wall. Light, chewy, and best eaten warm.

Jo — Barley Bread

A heartier, whole-grain loaf made with barley flour. Dense, nutty, and full of flavor, it's a traditional staple loved for its rustic texture and depth.

Fresh from the Tandoor

Baked daily in our traditional clay oven, heated by open flame. It’s the same time-honored method used in Iranian bakeries for centuries.

Our Story

A Tradition Baked in Clay

Naan is rooted in the long-standing tradition of Iranian bread, handmade and baked in a traditional tandoor. For generations, this method of baking has brought communities together around the simple ritual of fresh bread: dough shaped by hand, slapped against scorching clay, and pulled out moments later, blistered and fragrant.

We honor that tradition every day, baking our Taftoon and Jo breads the same way it has always been done: no shortcuts, no machines, just fire, clay, and hands that know the craft.
